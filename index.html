Learn crowd dynamics three times, each time deeper

Crowds link people, space, movement and risk. You will meet the whole system three times: first as a picture, then as models you build and test, then as a research tool for keeping mass gatherings such as the Hajj safe.

Go 1Introductory FoundationsUnderstand what a crowd is, how density, speed and flow are linked, why bottlenecks and high densities are dangerous, and how safe events are planned. 8 modules · about 4.8 hours · you need: nothingGo 2Intermediate Build and ImplementOpen the models crowd scientists use: measure crowds from data, then build macroscopic, cellular-automaton, social-force and evacuation models, and test them against data. 8 modules · about 5.9 hours · you need: go 1, or its exit checkGo 3Advanced Research FrontierDesign, run and defend a research study of a mass gathering, with the Hajj and large events as the running case. 8 modules · about 6.2 hours · you need: go 2, or its exit check

How the goes fit together

Start where you are

Each go opens with a three-question check. If you already know the answers, skip ahead. Each go closes with an exit check: tick off what you can do before moving on.

Each go works differently

Go 1 is pictures and predict-then-check explorers. Go 2 is connection maps and small models you build. Go 3 is a research brief for each step of a real mass-gathering study, with code.

Real science, honest toys

Facts and parameters come from the cited experiments, papers and guidance. Explorers and toy models are small teaching versions; their numbers are illustrative and say so. They are not design tools.

Facts follow the cited literature and guidance (Fruin, Weidmann, Helbing and colleagues, Seyfried and colleagues, Still, and the Green Guide, among others).

An open course on crowd dynamics and crowd safety, taught in three goes, with mass gatherings as the research case. Facts follow the cited literature and guidance (Fruin, Weidmann, Helbing and colleagues, Seyfried and colleagues, Still, and the Green Guide, among others). Explorers and toy models are teaching versions with illustrative numbers: never use them on their own to size or approve a real venue.
